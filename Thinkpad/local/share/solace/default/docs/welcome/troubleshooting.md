# Troubleshooting

When something looks off, the fastest fixes are usually service restarts.

## Restart desktop pieces

Open `Solace Menu > Update > Process`.

Useful entries:

- Waybar
- Walker
- Mako
- SwayOSD
- Hypridle
- Hyprsunset

## Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, And Audio

Open `Solace Menu > Setup` for the friendly controls.

Open `Solace Menu > Update > Hardware` to restart audio, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or
trackpad services.

## Keybindings

Press `Super + K` and search for what you want to do.
